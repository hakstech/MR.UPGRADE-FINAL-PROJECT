Customer Side

How customers use your website

This is what your customers see and do on your website:

1

Browse Products

Customers visit your site and browse iPhones, Samsung, Google Pixel, laptops and more. They can filter by category, condition and price.

2

Create an Account / Login

Customers must register with their name, email and phone number to place an order.

3

Add to Cart

Customers click "Add" on a product to add it to their cart. They can adjust quantities or remove items.

4

Choose Payment Type

At checkout, customers choose Full Payment or Installment Plan (40% deposit, rest over 10/12/16 weeks).

5

Choose Fulfillment

Customers choose to either Pick Up at Store (Pioneer Mall, Room PG52) or Delivery.

6

Pay via MTN or Airtel

Customer selects MTN MoMo or Airtel Money → gets redirected to Pesapal's secure payment page → enters number → receives USSD PIN prompt on phone → approves → money is deducted → order confirmed.

✅

Cash on Delivery is also available. The customer pays when they collect from the store or when delivered.

Managing Products

Add, edit and remove products from your store

How to Add a New Product

1

Go to Products

Click "Products" in the sidebar, then click the "+ Add Product" button at the top right.

2

Fill in the Details

Enter: Product Name, Category, Model, Storage, Color, Condition (New or UK Used), Price (UGX), Stock Quantity, and Description.

3

Upload a Photo

Click "Choose File" and select a photo from your computer. Max size: 20MB. The photo will show a preview before you save.

4

Mark as Featured (Optional)

Tick "Featured" if you want this product to appear on the homepage. Only featured products show on the home page.

5

Save

Click "Add Product". The product will immediately appear in the shop.

How to Edit a Product

In the Products list, click the ✏️ pen icon next to any product. Change what you need and click "Save Changes".

How to Remove a Product

Click the 🗑️ red trash icon next to a product. A confirmation popup will appear — click "Delete" to confirm. The product will be hidden from the shop immediately.

ℹ️

Tip: To temporarily hide a product without deleting it, edit it and uncheck "Active (visible on site)". It will be hidden from customers but still in your database.

Managing Orders

View, process and update customer orders

Order Status Meanings

PendingOrder placed, awaiting confirmationConfirm payment, change to Confirmed
ConfirmedPayment confirmedPrepare the item

ShippedOut for deliveryWait for delivery confirmation
DeliveredCustomer received itNothing — order complete ✅
CancelledOrder was cancelledCheck reason, contact customer if needed

How to Update an Order Status

1

Open the Order

Click "View" next to any order in the Orders list.

2

Scroll to "Update Order Status"

At the bottom of the order detail page, find the orange "Update Order Status" section.

3

Select New Status + Payment Status

Choose the new Order Status and Payment Status from the dropdowns, then click "Update".

💡

Payment Status: When a customer pays, it automatically updates. But for Cash on Delivery orders, you must manually change Payment Status to "Fully Paid" after they pay.

Installment Plans

Track customers paying in weekly installments

When a customer chooses the installment option, they pay 40% deposit now and the remaining balance in weekly payments over 10, 12 or 16 weeks.

How to Track Installment Payments

1

Go to Installments

Click "Installments" in the sidebar. You will see a list of all active installment plans.

2

Find the Customer's Plan

Each row shows: Order number, Customer name, Total amount, Deposit paid, Weekly payment amount, Progress bar, Status.

3

Update Weekly Payments

Click "View & Manage" → scroll to the Installment Plan section → update "Weeks Paid" number each time the customer makes a payment → click "Update Plan".

4

Mark as Completed

When all weeks are paid, change the Status to "Completed".

Installment StatusMeaning
ActiveCustomer is currently paying
CompletedAll payments made — fully paid
DefaultedCustomer missed payments

Customer Messages

Read and reply to customer contact messages

When a customer fills in the Contact form on your website, the message appears here. Unread messages are shown with a blue dot.

How to Read and Reply to a Message

1

Click on a Message

Click any message in the left panel. The full message appears on the right side.

2

Read the Message Details

You can see the customer's name, email, phone number, subject, and their full message.

3

Choose How to Reply

Scroll down to "Reply Via" and choose one of these:

Reply OptionHow it works
📧 Email / GmailOpens your email app pre-filled with the customer's address and subject. Click Send.
💬 WhatsAppOpens WhatsApp with the customer's number and a pre-written greeting. Click Send.
📱 Send SMSType a message (max 160 characters), click "Send SMS". Customer receives a text message on their phone.
ℹ️

SMS History: Every SMS you send is saved below the message. You can delete old SMS history entries by clicking the red ✕ button.

How to Delete a Message

Open the message → click the red "Delete" button at the top → confirm in the popup. The message is permanently removed.

My Account

Update your admin profile and change your password

Click "My Account" at the bottom of the left sidebar to manage your admin profile.

Profile Info Tab — Change Your Name or Email

1

Click "Profile Info" tab

It is selected by default (blue tab at the top of the form).

2

Edit Your Details

Change your name, email or phone number as needed.

3

Save

Click "Save Changes". The page will refresh and show your updated name in the top bar.

Change Password Tab

1

Click "Change Password" tab

The tab turns orange when selected.

2

Enter Your Current Password

For security, you must first confirm your current password.

3

Enter New Password Twice

Type your new password and confirm it. The strength bar will show if it is weak or strong. Use at least 8 characters with numbers and letters.

4

Click "Change Password"

Your password is updated immediately.

Important Tips & Security

Best practices for keeping your site safe and running smoothly

🔒

Never share your admin password with anyone. The admin panel gives full control of your website, products, and customer data.

⚠️

Change your password regularly — at least every 3 months. Use a strong password with letters, numbers and symbols.

ℹ️

Always log out when using the admin panel on a shared or public computer. Click "Logout" at the bottom of the sidebar.
