### 1. Business Overview
MR UPGRADE is a smartphone and gadget retail business based at Pioneer Mall, Room PG52, Kampala, Uganda. The business sells genuine, unlocked smartphones including iPhones (XR through 16 Pro Max), Samsung Galaxy series, Google Pixel, and laptops (MacBook, HP, Dell, Lenovo). A key differentiator is the **flexible installment plan** — customers pay 40% deposit and spread the balance over 10, 12 or 16 weekly payments.

### 2. Goals
1. Allow customers to browse all products with prices online
2. Enable online ordering with MTN MoMo and Airtel Money payments
3. Manage installment plans digitally — reducing manual WhatsApp-based processing

### 3. Target Audience
Young adults aged 18–35 in Kampala and surrounding areas, primarily using smartphones, communicating in English.

### 4. Scope
**In scope:**
- Multi-page responsive e-commerce website
- Product catalogue (66+ products, 14 categories)
- User registration, login, cart and checkout
- Installment plan with 40% deposit and weekly payment tracking
- MTN MoMo and Airtel Money payments via Pesapal
- Admin panel (products, orders, customers, messages, installments)
- SMS reply system via Africa's Talking
- SEO, security hardening, sitemap

**Out of scope:**
- Native mobile app
- Multi-language support
- Third-party accounting integration

### A. Admin Guide — How to Manage the Website

#### Logging In
1. Go to: **https://mrupgrade.mmuhub.com/login**
2. Enter your admin email and password
3. You are taken directly to the Admin Dashboard

#### Admin Dashboard
Shows total orders, revenue, customers and products at a glance. Low stock products are highlighted in orange.

#### Managing Products
- **Add product:** Admin → Products → + Add Product → fill in name, category, price, stock, image → Save
- **Edit product:** Click the ✏️ pen icon next to any product → change details → Save
- **Remove product:** Click the 🗑️ trash icon → confirm in popup

#### Managing Orders
- **View orders:** Admin → Orders → click any order to see full details
- **Update status:** Open order → scroll to "Update Order Status" → select status → Update
- **Cash payments:** Change Payment Status to "Fully Paid" after cash is received

#### Installment Plans
- Admin → Installments → click "View & Manage" on any plan
- Update "Weeks Paid" each time a customer makes a weekly payment
- Change status to "Completed" when fully paid

#### Replying to Messages
- Admin → Messages → click any message → Reply Via Email, WhatsApp or SMS

#### Changing Password
- Admin → My Account → Change Password tab → enter current and new password → Save
